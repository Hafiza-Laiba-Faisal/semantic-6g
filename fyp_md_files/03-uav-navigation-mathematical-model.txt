H. Communication-Aware UAV Control
Paper 4 (Shiri et al., 2019) - Section III
Communication affects control through:
Downlink delay (state transmission):
D_{\text{DL},k} = t_{\text{DL},k} - t_{o,k}
Uplink delay (action/model transmission):
D_{\text{UL},k} = t_{\text{UL},k} - t_{\text{DL},k}
End-to-end delay:
D_{\text{E2E},k} = D_{\text{DL},k} + D_{\text{UL},k} = t_{\text{UL},k} - t_{o,k}
Delay model (Eq. 10):
D_{i,k} = \frac{b_i}{W_i \log_2\left(1 + \frac{P_i \cdot 10^{-L(t_{o,k})/10}}{N_i W_i}\right)}
where:
b_i: Payload size (bits)
W_i: Bandwidth (Hz)
P_i: Transmit power (W)
L(t): Path loss (dB)
N_i: Noise spectral density (W/Hz)
Path loss model (Eq. 11-12): 3GPP urban-micro UAV channel model with LOS probability
Explicitly given: Yes, Eq. 10-12.
Mathematical relationship: Communication delay causes outdated state/action information, degrading control performance

Paper 5 (Fang et al., 2025) - Section II.B
Communication model:
Shannon capacity (Eq. 2):
R = B \log_2\left(1 + \frac{P \cdot g}{N_0 \cdot B}\right)
Channel gain:
g = g_0 \cdot \left(\frac{d_0}{d}\right)^\alpha \cdot 10^{\frac{\xi}{10}} \cdot |h|^2
where:
