## 1. Question

Wave-A and Wave-C showed that adding control and velocity penalties
(`λ_u = λ_v = 0.1`) takes the task arm from 0.02–0.05 to ≈1.0. They did not
show **which** term does the work. Starting from the λ-heavy arm
`(λ_d, λ_u, λ_v, λ_T) = (1.0, 0.1, 0.1, 2.0)`, one term is removed at a time.

## 3. Answer: the **control-effort penalty `λ_u` is the active term**

- Removing `λ_u` collapses success to 0.016–0.027 and raises the final loss by
  one to two orders of magnitude (305 → 5,214).
- Removing `λ_v` or `λ_T` changes nothing measurable (0.992–0.999, i.e. within
  the ceiling).
- The `m5_no_u`, k = 18 AWGN cell additionally **destabilises**: final loss
  5,213.5 with `grads_finite = False` at the final step — the same failure mode
  as the M2 conditional-accept cells (A-1). It is a **declared failure** (A-18)
  and was **not retrained**.

**Interpretation.** The λ-shaping effect is not a generic "more terms is
better" effect. It is a single-term effect: the control-effort penalty is what
keeps the encoder gradient bounded. The velocity and terminal terms are, at
this operating point, cosmetic. This sharpens the mechanism claim from
*"λ works"* to *"λ_u is the term that works, and removing it reproduces the
original failure"*.

**A mechanistic caveat.** `L_u` is the only term that is a direct function of
the *action* applied to the plant, so it is the only term that shapes the
encoder gradient through the closed loop. That is consistent with the
gradient-conditioning evidence in `RESULTS_MECHANISM_2026-10-27.md` and is the
plausible causal story — but this experiment localises the effect, it does not
prove the causal chain.

## 5. What this changes in the thesis

1. **§5.3 / §6.2** can now say *which* term matters, not just that λ matters.
2. **§6.2 mechanism paragraph** gains one sentence: the control penalty is the
   only term that acts on the action, which is why it is the one that bounds
   the encoder gradient.
3. **§7** gains a line: `λ_v` and `λ_T` were not shown to be necessary at this
   operating point; they were not removed from the primary arm.
4. **Do not claim** that λ_v/λ_T are harmful — they are simply not load-bearing
   in this experiment.
