# C. Task representation

For Task-Oriented DeepJSCC:

$$
\boxed{
q_t=
[x_g-x_t,\;
y_g-y_t,\;
v_{x,t},\;
v_{y,t}]
}
$$

So:

$$
q_t\in\mathbb R^4.
$$

The decoder produces:

$$
\hat q_t=g_\phi(y_t).
$$

Then the fixed controller operates on \(\hat q_t\):

$$
\boxed{
\tilde u_t=
K_p\hat e_{p,t}-K_d\hat v_t.
}
$$

**Status:** project-specific design choice, not a literature-prescribed numerical parameter.

# E. Power normalization

For transmitted complex vector \(z\):

$$
z\leftarrow
\sqrt{
\frac{n_cP}
{\|z\|_2^2+\epsilon}
}z
$$

so that approximately:

$$
\frac{1}{n_c}\|z\|_2^2=P.
$$

For implementation:

$$
\boxed{P=1}
$$

is a convenient **normalization convention**, not a physical UAV transmit-power claim.

---

# F. AWGN

$$
y=z+n
$$

with

$$
n\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,\sigma_n^2I).
$$

With \(P=1\):

$$
\boxed{
\sigma_n^2=10^{-\mathrm{SNR}_{dB}/10}
}
$$

and each real I/Q component uses

$$
\boxed{
\sigma_{\mathrm{I/Q}}
=
\sqrt{\frac{\sigma_n^2}{2}}.
}
$$

---

# G. Rayleigh fading

$$
\boxed{
y=hz+n
}
$$

where

$$
h\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,1).
$$

v1:

* block fading
* one \(h\) per transmission
* independent realization between transmissions
* perfect CSIR for the main experiment
* equalization before decoder

$$
\tilde y=\frac{y}{h+\epsilon}.
$$

Imperfect CSI = **ablation**, not core experiment.

# K. Task loss

We will initially implement a weighted differentiable rollout loss:

$$
\boxed{
L_{task}
=
\lambda_pL_p+
\lambda_vL_v+
\lambda_uL_u+
\lambda_TL_T
}
$$

where

$$
L_p=
\frac1T
\sum_t
\|p_t-p_g\|^2
$$

$$
L_v=
\frac1T
\sum_t
\|v_t\|^2
$$

$$
L_u=
\frac1T
\sum_t
\|u_t\|^2
$$

and

$$
L_T=
\|p_T-p_g\|^2.
$$

### Numerical weights

$$
\lambda_p,\lambda_v,\lambda_u,\lambda_T
$$

remain **TBD**.

We should **not arbitrarily set them to 1** yet because their relative scaling can materially change training.
